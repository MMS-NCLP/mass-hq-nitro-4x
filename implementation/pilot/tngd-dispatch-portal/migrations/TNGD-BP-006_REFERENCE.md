# TNGD-BP-006 Persistence Reference

An authorized implementation shall create tenant-owned technician profile, recurring shift, distinct service-capability and technician-skill, service-area, exception, capacity override, and capacity-event tables with UUID defaults, `UNIQUE(id, tenant_id)`, composite tenant-safe references, exclusion constraints for incompatible intervals, immutable audit evidence, and `auth.jwt()` RLS.

Profile persistence shall enforce finite non-negative travel radii; positive-integer daily limits; and non-negative-integer same-day and emergency limits. Override persistence shall enforce a non-negative-integer capacity amount and retain immutable identifier, effective date, reason, and author evidence. Availability exceptions shall support all-day and bounded intervals. Capacity-query boundaries shall require finite non-negative travel distance, and date-scoped capacity projections shall count only appointments on the requested date. This reference does not authorize a database provider or deployed migration.
