# BP-010 Lifecycle and Business Rules

Requests move from presented to approved, declined, expired, revoked, or superseded. Approved and declined decision evidence is immutable. Revocation adds evidence without deleting the original approval. Changed scope or price requires a new finalized BP-009 version and a new authorization request linked by an amendment.

Approval requires an authorized-adult acknowledgment, approver identity or governed access, presented-content hash, decision timestamp and method, and signature or approved equivalent evidence. Only current approval produces a BP-011-ready reference handoff.
