# BP-006 Technician Availability and Capacity

BP-006 creates tenant-owned Technician Availability Profiles, Capacity Windows, Availability Exceptions, and Technician Capability Matrices. Profiles govern shifts, service capabilities, areas, travel radius, workload limits, emergency controls, equipment, and vehicles. Exceptions cover PTO, blackout, training, and administrative holds. Reasoned overrides are temporary, attributed, and audited.
