# BP-008 25-Point Inspection Template and Validation Rules

The diagnostic is named **25-Point Inspection**. The source checklist contains 19 itemized component checks:

1. Springs
2. Cables
3. Rollers
4. Hinges and Hardware
5. Pulleys or Drums
6. End Bearings
7. Center Bearing or Plate
8. Top and Bottom Brackets
9. Jamb Brackets
10. Vertical Tracks
11. Horizontal Tracks
12. Panels and Vinyl Trim
13. Bottom Rubber or Retainer
14. Operator
15. Rail, Trolley, Chain, or Belt
16. Safety Sensors
17. Wiring, Force, and Travel Limits
18. Keypad, Remotes, and Wall Button
19. Leveled Ground

Every component accepts exactly one of: **Does Not Apply**, **Pass**, **Flag**, or **Fail**. The completed customer-facing report retains the complete itemized list. Flag and Fail results become reference-only BP-009 findings.

The branded experience also captures notes, measurements, door quantity, spring identification, door size, spring type, ground-level condition, before/diagnostic/after media references, sticker and warranty-disclosure confirmation, and referral-card confirmation. These are operational evidence fields, not additional Pass/Flag/Fail component checks.

The inspection is required for Repair and optional for Estimate. When an inspection is performed, submission requires all 19 results, complete door details, before and after photograph references, warranty-disclosure confirmation, and referral-card confirmation. Missing evidence blocks submission. The detailed garage-door order form remains deferred.
