# MASS Dispatch V2 — Adaptive Ambient Dynamic Pulse

**Class:** V2 Product Objective / Deferred Enhancement  
**Status:** PRESERVED — DO NOT EXECUTE DURING V1  
**Origin:** TNGD-UI-PR-001A and Executive product-direction session  
**Product:** MASS Dispatch

## Objective

Evolve the V1 Operational Pulse baseline into a governed visual-intelligence system that communicates operational health, urgency, condition, recovery and vitality without manufacturing anxiety or visual burnout.

## Governing Principle

**MASS communicates condition through vitality rather than alarm.**

Urgency must be unmistakable without becoming hysterical. As consequences become more serious, the system should become calmer, clearer and more deliberate.

## Preserved V2 Objectives

### Operational Pulse

Operational Pulse becomes the primary ambient health/condition surface for whatever operational context it is presenting. “Stage” may remain design shorthand for the canvas; Operational Pulse is the product capability.

### Five distinguishable pulse conditions

Develop and validate approximately five semantically named conditions, conceptually:

**Steady → Aware → Strained → Weak → Critical**

Names and thresholds require V2 product validation. Do not reduce these states to generic traffic-light colors.

### Continuous MASS Blue → Nitro 4X Red spectrum

MASS Blue establishes homeostasis: alive, monitored, steady. As condition degrades, the same visual organism gradually shifts toward the approved Nitro 4X red family. Avoid abrupt blue/purple/yellow/orange/red substitution schemes.

Even Critical should retain visual continuity with MASS. Nothing visually “dies”; the pulse becomes unmistakably weak/serious while stewardship remains present.

### Multi-dimensional pulse grammar

Investigate distinct visual dimensions rather than equating faster animation with greater urgency:

- **Rate:** activity/change frequency
- **Strength:** health/vitality
- **Gradient position:** condition severity
- **Amplitude/glow depth:** attention relevance
- **Persistence:** unresolved duration

Serious conditions should not automatically become faster, louder or more saturated.

### Supporting-card inheritance

Supporting/priority cards may inherit the Pulse language at substantially reduced expressive strength. Operational Pulse owns the environmental signal. Priority cards should remain materially quieter; ordinary/static informational surfaces should generally remain still unless their state changes.

### Coordinated event propagation

Where a domain event affects multiple visible surfaces, create a coherent visual response so one business event reads as one system event. Example: JOB_COMPLETED may update technician state, completion count, revenue where applicable, map state, recent activity and the next Operational Pulse context in coordinated fashion.

This must be driven by real events/state, not timed decorative animation.

### Adaptive operational-health intelligence

Evaluate a governed Dispatch Pulse State derived from relevant operational conditions such as schedule risk, unassigned workload, overdue follow-ups, authorization backlog, technician utilization, response latency, payment exceptions, integration/system health and revenue trajectory.

Do not expose a simplistic score unless it genuinely improves comprehension. The purpose is ambient understanding, not gamification.

### Explainable pulse

Where intelligence influences Pulse condition, MASS should be able to explain why. Example: “Operations are elevated because two afternoon jobs are at schedule risk; revenue and system health remain normal.”

### Resolution and recovery language

Define recovery transitions with the same care as degradation. The system should visibly regain vitality as conditions resolve rather than merely clearing alerts.

### MASS product-family primitive

Evaluate Pulse as a reusable MASS design primitive across products while preserving product-specific semantics: Operational Pulse for Dispatch; Organizational Pulse for HQ; Production Pulse for Studio; Growth/Market Pulse for growth surfaces; and an appropriately quieter personal/attention expression for future MASS Life.

## Psychological / Stewardship Rules

- MASS shall not manufacture anxiety to communicate urgency.
- Critical information becomes clearer and more composed, not more frantic.
- Ambient signals communicate condition; explicit content communicates cause and authorized action.
- No casino behavior, decorative perpetual motion or attention competition between every card.
- Accessibility, reduced-motion preferences, contrast, performance and field usability are mandatory design inputs.
- Motion and color cannot be the sole carriers of meaning.

## V2 Discovery / Engineering Questions

Before implementation, determine whether the existing event/state architecture can support coordinated propagation without unnecessary coupling; define performance budgets; define reduced-motion behavior; establish pulse-state semantics and thresholds; validate color/contrast behavior; test whether users correctly perceive weakening vitality versus urgency; and confirm that the system remains useful under dense real-world TNGD operating conditions.

## V2 Exit Intent

The objective is not a more animated dashboard. The objective is a cockpit whose ambient behavior helps an operator perceive the health and changing condition of the business before manually interrogating every card, while MASS remains calm enough to preserve trust and attention.

**Deferred directive: Give the cockpit a cardiovascular system only after V1 proves the body in the field.**
