# ENGINEERING WORK ORDER
## EWO-MASS-APP-014-V14

**Project:** MASS

**Application:** APP-014 — Creative & Knowledge Intelligence

**Volume:** V14

**Title:** Policy Intelligence, Governance Advisory & Compliance Reasoning

**Status:** Approved for Manufacturing

**Target:** Production Baseline v1.0

## Mission

Manufacture the governed intelligence responsible for interpreting organizational policy, constitutional rules, engineering standards, and operational governance in order to provide explainable advisory guidance without assuming executive authority.

This volume shall unify policy awareness across the MASS platform while preserving human decision-making.

V14 shall become the platform's authoritative advisory layer for constitutional compliance, engineering governance, policy interpretation, and operational consistency.

## Scope

### Policy Intelligence

Define:

- Policy registry
- Policy classification
- Policy versioning
- Policy applicability
- Policy precedence
- Policy relationships
- Effective dates
- Policy retirement

### Governance Reasoning

Support:

- Constitutional interpretation
- Engineering standard lookup
- Application rule lookup
- Organizational policy lookup
- Conflict identification
- Exception analysis
- Governance impact assessment

### Advisory Intelligence

Generate:

- Compliance recommendations
- Policy impact summaries
- Conflict explanations
- Missing policy detection
- Governance risk indicators
- Executive advisory briefs

Recommendations shall always include supporting evidence and traceability.

### Compliance Awareness

Evaluate:

- Constitutional compliance
- Engineering compliance
- Application compliance
- Organizational policy compliance
- Tenant-specific governance
- Operational consistency

V14 shall identify deviations but shall not independently correct them.

### Platform Consumption

Consume:

- MASS Constitution
- Engineering Library
- APP-013
- APP-014 V01–V13
- Organizational policies
- Template Library
- Information Lineage
- Analytics

Produce:

- Governance recommendations
- Compliance findings
- Advisory reports
- Policy citations
- Executive governance briefings

## Human Authority

V14 may:

- Interpret
- Explain
- Recommend
- Compare
- Prioritize

V14 shall never:

- Change policy
- Override executive decisions
- Modify constitutional rules
- Approve governance changes
- Enforce disciplinary action
- Execute compliance actions

All governance actions require human authorization.
