**Project:** MASS

**Application:** APP-014 — Creative & Knowledge Intelligence

**Title:** Continuous Organizational Learning & Improvement Intelligence

## Mission

Manufacture the intelligence responsible for transforming completed organizational work into reusable knowledge that continuously improves future execution.

This volume shall define how MASS learns from outcomes without becoming autonomous. Learning is evidence-based, tenant-scoped, reviewable, and advisory. Human authority remains absolute.

Capture the complete long-term architectural vision while clearly distinguishing Permanent Architecture from V1 Implementation.

## Human Authority

Learning may recommend.

Learning may explain.

Learning may prioritize.

Learning shall never:

- approve
- modify organizational policy
- rewrite historical records
- execute operational changes
- create autonomous feedback loops

All organizational improvements require human approval.
