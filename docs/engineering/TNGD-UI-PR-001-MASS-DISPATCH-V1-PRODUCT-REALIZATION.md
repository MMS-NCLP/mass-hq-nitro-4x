# TNGD-UI-PR-001 — MASS Dispatch V1 Product Realization Pass

**Class:** Final Product Realization / UX Conformance  
**Status:** AUTHORIZED — HOLD  
**Execution position:** After V1 functional completion, launch-critical integrations, and White-Glove pilot-readiness validation  
**Product:** MASS Dispatch  
**Pilot tenant:** Top Notch Garage Doors

## Purpose

Transform the functionally complete, pilot-ready MASS Dispatch V1 into the approved MASS Dispatch product experience.

This is not a new architecture phase, feature-expansion phase, backend redesign, roadmap extension, MASS HQ redesign, or open-ended aesthetic exploration. The machine is presumed operational. This pass exists to make the machine reveal its capability, intelligence, hierarchy, state, evidence and available action with the clarity and visual authority established by approved governance, specifications, assets and visual references.

## Entry Conditions

Do not execute until the existing V1 functional roadmap is complete; launch-critical integrations are dispositioned/implemented; backend/frontend gates are green; security and permissions are validated; White-Glove functional audit is complete; critical dead ends are resolved; and V1 is independently capable of controlled pilot operation.

At entry: **freeze V1 architecture.** Only critical security, permission, data-integrity or workflow-breaking defects justify architectural correction during realization.

## Governing Standard

**NO MORE. NO LESS.**

Production pauses here only to gain the defined advantages of comprehension, visual hierarchy, signal authority, interaction quality, information density, product identity, operational confidence, visual representation and contextual depth. Every approved advantage must be realized; speculative invention is prohibited.

Read before implementation:

1. MASS Visual Comprehension & Product Realization Doctrine
2. applicable MASS Dispatch master directive and V1 specifications
3. DESIGN.md / current design system
4. approved MASS Dispatch visual references and reference index
5. authoritative brand assets
6. role/permission model
7. current functional implementation
8. this work order

Actual approved visual references are implementation authority, not casual inspiration. Do not blindly hard-code screenshots. Extract and implement their system: **hierarchy → density → segmentation → signal → interaction → depth → identity.**

## Required Surface Conformance

Normalize existing V1 surfaces against the approved system, including Today/Operations Pulse, Technician Today, Customers, Customer Detail/Edit, Intake, Schedule/Calendar, Dispatch, Jobs, Diagnosis, Inspection, Estimates, Authorization, Invoices/Payment, Warranty, Follow-Up, Admin Lifecycle Pipeline, Reports, Administration, Settings/Integrations, and applicable authentication/error/empty surfaces.

Do not add functionality merely to fill visual space. Do not remove working functionality because it complicates composition.

## Product Rules

High-authority command surfaces may use the established dark MASS treatment. Working/field surfaces remain predominantly lighter and operational. Use dimensional gray/off-white/lifted surfaces deliberately; do not whitewash them.

Important visible information should follow **signal → context → evidence → record → authorized action**. A visual element that implies depth but does nothing is a defect. A summary that cannot expose available evidence is incomplete.

Operational information should paint a picture. Where supported, provide appropriate filtering, sorting, comparison, time ranges, drill-down, print behavior, responsive tables and meaningful visual analysis. Do not force every dataset into the same visualization.

Signals gain authority through restraint. Routine information must not visually compete with prudent information.

## Responsive & Real-Data Validation

Validate desktop, practical tablet widths, practical mobile widths, touch targets, long forms, scrolling, sticky controls where justified, keyboard/input behavior, field readability, bright-environment contrast and print layouts where applicable.

Stress-test realistic difficult states: long names/addresses/notes, missing optional data, large histories, dense and empty schedules, overloaded calendar days, overdue follow-ups, dense pipelines, zero/high revenue, failed payment, refund/adjustment, warranty claims, disconnected/error integrations, no-data/loading/permission-denied states and hundreds of records where applicable.

Question: **Does MASS Dispatch remain composed?** Fix presentation failures without redesigning architecture.

## Execution Method

**Pass 1 — Realization:** implement the complete approved product specification across V1 surfaces.

**Pass 2 — Conformance:** compare implementation against approved visual references, design system, UX doctrine, surface specifications and this WO; correct discrepancies.

**Pass 3 — Field Polish:** resolve responsive defects, overflow, contrast, density, awkward interaction, inconsistent states, touch problems, print defects and real-data presentation failures.

Then stop.

## Prohibited Drift

Do not redesign backend domains; introduce speculative architecture; add roadmap phases; manufacture V2 features; redesign MASS HQ; turn Dispatch into MASS HQ; invent new AI systems; add decorative animation; refactor unrelated working systems; redesign approved brand identity; create functionality merely because space exists; or continue polishing indefinitely.

Record noncritical out-of-authority improvements for V1.x/V2 and keep moving.

## Exception Rule

Interrupt realization only for a security defect, permission-boundary defect, data-integrity defect, critical workflow failure, or implementation defect preventing an approved V1 capability from operating. Apply the smallest controlled correction required.

## Acceptance Standard

The pass succeeds when approved visual hierarchy is recognizable throughout; MASS Dispatch has coherent product identity; TNGD is clearly represented as tenant/client identity; important signals have authority; working areas remain efficient; visual representations improve comprehension; major information is drillable; forms remain operationally fast; dashboards communicate rather than decorate; dense information remains composed; real data survives the design; field use remains credible; print/report surfaces behave appropriately; no critical functionality regresses; security/permissions remain intact; and the full regression gate remains green.

## Exit Condition

After Pass 3, run final regression and produce **MASS Dispatch V1 — Product Realization & Pilot Conformance Report** covering surfaces realized, visual-reference conformance, responsive validation, real-data stress results, functional regression, permission/security regression, known cosmetic limitations, deferred V1.x/V2 improvements and final pilot recommendation.

If no launch-critical defect remains: declare Product Realization complete, stop, and release MASS Dispatch V1 to the controlled TNGD field pilot. Real-world operating evidence becomes the authority for subsequent refinement.

## Final Executive Directive

**Do not redesign the machine. Reveal it.**  
**Do not decorate information. Make it comprehensible.**  
**Do not make everything important. Make importance unmistakable.**  
**Realize the approved product. No more. No less.**
