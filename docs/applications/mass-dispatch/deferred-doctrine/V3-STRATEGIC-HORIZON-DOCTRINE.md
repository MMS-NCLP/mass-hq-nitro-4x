# MASS Dispatch — V3+ Strategic Horizon Doctrine

Status: STRATEGIC HORIZON / NOT V1 OR V2 AUTHORITY
Date: 2026-08-22

## Purpose

This record preserves higher-order capabilities so they are not lost while preventing them from contaminating V1/V2 scope.

## Strategic Horizons

### 1. Advanced Intelligence Layer

Higher-order operational intelligence may include predictive workload/capacity analysis, increasingly sophisticated lead prioritization, follow-up timing recommendations, service-demand forecasting, anomaly detection, operational recommendations, and governed automation.

Human authority and explainability remain central where customer commitments, money, scheduling, authorization, safety, or material business consequences are involved.

### 2. Advanced Workflow Intelligence

Potential future capabilities include adaptive queue ordering, learned-but-governed routing assistance, cross-object dependency awareness, sophisticated escalation, workload balancing, lifecycle optimization, and measured flywheel analysis across inquiry → conversion → service → post-service → referral/return.

### 3. Custom Business Intelligence / Report Construction

A mature custom report builder, advanced dashboard composition, cross-dimensional analytics, saved report definitions, scheduled delivery, forecasting models, and deeper executive intelligence belong here unless a narrower subset is deliberately promoted to V2.

### 4. Marketing Intelligence — Product Boundary Required

Potential MASS capabilities may include advanced source attribution, offer performance, review/referral intelligence, customer reactivation recommendations, promotion performance, and service-demand marketing signals.

Before expanding, explicitly protect the boundary with Momentum Marketing Solutions and any dedicated marketing software product. MASS should provide operational marketing intelligence, not silently become a full marketing agency platform.

### 5. Broader Financial Capabilities — Reconsider Before Building

Banking, business financing, consumer-financing marketplaces, insurance, tax administration, generalized expense accounting, payroll/accounting replacement, and payout-account management are not presumed MASS responsibilities.

Integrate with specialist providers when needed. Build native capability only after a separate product-boundary and economic justification.

### 6. Deeper Integration Ecosystem

Future integrations may support richer bidirectional synchronization, provider-specific automations, broader marketplace lead ingestion, advanced accounting, supplier/manufacturer ordering, communications intelligence, customer identity reconciliation, and operational partner ecosystems.

### 7. Mature Settings / Policy Engine

V3 may evolve V2 settings into a policy/configuration engine with versioning, environment/tenant controls, permission-aware configuration, rule simulation, audit history, staged rollout, and safer administrator customization.

## Horizon Rule

Nothing in this document is an implementation instruction. Promotion requires a deliberate architecture/work-order decision based on a stable preceding version, validated operational need, provider reality, and product-boundary review.
