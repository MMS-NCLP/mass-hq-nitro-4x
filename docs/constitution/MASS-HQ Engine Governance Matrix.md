## Purpose

This matrix is the authoritative constitutional reference mapping every Enterprise Engine to its governing department, executive oversight structure, constitutional purpose, primary entities, AI participation model, supporting departments, and engineering reference.

Enterprise Engines provide constitutional capability. Constitutional Departments provide governance. Executive Offices provide oversight. This matrix defines those relationships permanently.

## Constitutional Governance Summary

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Departments govern | Every engine has exactly one governing department |
| Engines enable | Engines provide capability; they do not set policy |
| Executive Offices oversee | Nova and Pops receive intelligence from every engine through its governing department |
| AI assists | AI participation is defined per engine; AI never governs |
| Independence is significance, not reclassification | Engines with independent volumes (V13, V14, V15) remain engines |
| Collaboration is constitutional | Supporting departments are defined per engine; no engine operates in isolation |
