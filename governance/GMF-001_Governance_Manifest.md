# GMF-001 — Governance Manifest

## Purpose

This manifest is the authoritative index of all governing artifacts within the MASS ecosystem. It answers one question:

**What currently constitutes the governing body of this ecosystem?**

This document contains no governing principles, no policy, and no architectural guidance. It is a registry. Governing authority resides in the documents it indexes, not in this manifest.

Every new governing artifact shall register itself here as part of its completion criteria. Every superseded or archived artifact shall have its status updated here.

## Change Control

This manifest is a living operational document. It shall be updated whenever a governing artifact is created, approved, superseded, or archived.

Updates to the manifest do not require formal revision authority — they are registry entries, not governance changes. The manifest records what exists; it does not authorize what may be created.

If the manifest conflicts with the metadata in an individual governing document, the individual document's metadata governs for that document.
