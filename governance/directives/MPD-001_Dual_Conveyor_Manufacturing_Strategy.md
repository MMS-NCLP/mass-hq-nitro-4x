# MASS PRODUCTION DIRECTIVE

## MPD-001 — Dual Conveyor Manufacturing Strategy

**Project:** MASS
**Authority:** Executive Production Directive
**Status:** Active
**Effective:** Immediately

---

## Executive Summary

MASS has reached the point where platform manufacturing and operational implementation may proceed simultaneously without violating architectural governance.

Effective immediately, production shall operate as two coordinated manufacturing conveyors.

Both conveyors support the same platform.

Neither conveyor supersedes the other.

Both remain governed by the MASS Constitution, Engineering Library, Product Roadmap, implementation reviews, and Executive Authority.

# Relationship Between Conveyors

Platform Manufacturing creates reusable capability.

Operational Manufacturing consumes reusable capability.

Operational Manufacturing may identify:

Missing capability

Missing API

Missing security requirement

Missing governance

Missing workflow

Missing documentation

These findings shall be returned to Platform Manufacturing through governed review rather than bypassing architecture.

The Operational Pilot shall never redefine platform architecture.

---

# Architectural Priority

When a required pilot capability already exists within the roadmap:

Consume it.

Do not duplicate it.

When a capability does not yet exist:

Determine whether:

A)

Platform Manufacturing should produce it first.

or

B)

The pilot should implement a narrowly scoped production version that conforms to the permanent architecture.

The pilot shall never create disposable software.

Every implementation must remain reusable by MASS.
