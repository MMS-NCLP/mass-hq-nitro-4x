## 1. Purpose

This audit exists so deferred MASS responsibilities do not depend on chat memory, individual developer recollection, or informal future notes.

It reconciles the newly established `governance/deferred/` registry against the existing canonical MASS roadmap and the TNGD Dispatch pilot backlog.

The audit distinguishes four classes:

1. **Active V1 obligations** — may not be pushed into V2 merely because implementation is difficult or incomplete.
2. **Approved V2/V3 doctrines** — version-classified in the deferred registry.
3. **Existing MASS platform roadmap responsibilities** — already preserved canonically, but not necessarily assigned to Dispatch V2 or V3.
4. **Historical candidates requiring provenance recovery** — concepts remembered from prior planning but not independently verified in the currently accessible canonical sources.

## 12. External-platform boundary preserved

MASS remains an operating/coordinating system, not a mandate to rebuild every specialist product.

The roadmap explicitly preserves coordination with CRM/service platforms, design systems, social/content systems, advertising platforms, email/communications systems, payment/appointment systems, accounting, storage/collaboration, analytics/reporting, and industry-specific tools.

This boundary supports the current Dispatch doctrine that Growth Intelligence may measure and operationalize lifecycle growth without turning MASS Dispatch into a full general-purpose marketing platform.

## 13. Square replacement boundary

Current canonical Dispatch scope treats Square as the delegated payment processor while MASS maintains its own financial state. The pilot backlog explicitly requires Square integration and prohibits storage of restricted card data.

Therefore, **replacing Square is not current V1 doctrine**.

Any future concept in which MASS replaces parts of Square must be treated as a separately governed financial-platform decision after Dispatch invoicing/payment has been proven in real operation. It must not be inferred from the existence of MASS invoices or payments.

## 14. Webhook / REST / event integration boundary

The canonical roadmap already preserves external API/gateway contracts, event subscriptions/publications, Integration Marketplace responsibilities, synchronization, and connector lifecycle.

Therefore future webhook/REST expansion should be developed through those provider-neutral platform contracts rather than as ungoverned one-off endpoints.

Where an earlier chat described a generic future webhook/REST integration architecture, its architectural intent is consistent with existing roadmap responsibilities, but this audit does not claim a separate historical doctrine was recovered unless its original source artifact is located.

## 15. Media / AI extension boundary

Current and planned MASS architecture already preserves governed media, organizational memory, AI-assisted creativity/intelligence, plugins, agents, and future learning.

The correct future-proofing principle is:

> V1/V2 may preserve media metadata, provenance, event hooks, stable identifiers, and provider-neutral seams needed by later intelligence; they should not prematurely claim an AI capability is implemented merely because hook points exist.

Any earlier discussion of "AI/media hook points" should be interpreted through this boundary unless an original doctrine is later recovered.

## 16. Behavioral event history / retention boundary

V3 intelligence requires trustworthy history, while APP-022 and the roadmap's knowledge/governance responsibilities preserve retention and audit concerns.

Accordingly, structured operational events should be retained according to governed privacy/security/retention policy sufficient to support audit and authorized future intelligence.

This does **not** authorize indefinite raw behavioral surveillance or unlimited retention. Exact retention schedules require explicit policy.

## 18. Recovery result

The deferred registry now has two distinct responsibilities:

1. **Version-specific doctrine** for approved V2, V3, and future MASS Dispatch evolution.
2. **Historical preservation/reconciliation** for broader MASS platform responsibilities and older deferred concepts.
