# MASS V3 Reference Doctrine — Predictive Intelligence

**Doctrine ID:** MASS-V3-DOCTRINE-001  
**Target:** MASS Dispatch V3  
**Classification:** Deferred Product Doctrine / Intelligence Reference  
**Status:** Approved Reference Baseline  
**Established:** 2026-08-22

## 1. Generational purpose

V3 is the intelligence generation of MASS.

**V1 — Observe + Execute:** establish reliable operational truth and complete workflows.  
**V2 — Govern + Optimize:** make those workflows configurable, policy-driven, auditable, automated where appropriate, and operationally optimized.  
**V3 — Learn + Anticipate:** use trustworthy accumulated history, outcomes, configuration, and current state to predict, recommend, and warn before conditions fully materialize.

V3 shall not be used to excuse incomplete V1 functionality or missing V2 governance.

## 2. Foundational dependency

V3 intelligence depends on trustworthy structured history. Before learning-based behavior is given operational influence, MASS should possess reliable records of sources, states, signals, actions, outcomes, next actions, ownership, timestamps, communications, estimates, jobs, payments, customer lifecycle events, configuration, rule execution, and relevant operational outcomes.

**Capture truth first. Govern it second. Learn from it third.**

## 3. Predictive Pipeline intelligence

V3 may introduce capabilities such as:

- conversion probability;
- optimal contact-window recommendations;
- source quality scoring;
- customer responsiveness indicators;
- recommended next action based on historical outcomes;
- dormant opportunity detection;
- likelihood of estimate authorization;
- lifecycle re-entry recommendations;
- learned prioritization signals.

Predictions should supplement, not silently overwrite, governed Pipeline state.

## 4. Predictive Dispatch intelligence

V3 may learn from historical technician/job outcomes to improve:

- technician/job affinity;
- predicted completion time;
- skill/service matching;
- route and assignment recommendations;
- workload risk;
- schedule overrun probability;
- downstream appointment risk;
- field capacity forecasting.

Human authority remains the default for material dispatch decisions unless a later doctrine explicitly authorizes bounded autonomy.

## 5. Demand, capacity, and geographic forecasting

V3 may provide predictive models for:

- seasonal demand;
- future appointment pressure;
- staffing/capacity requirements;
- geographic service demand;
- likely schedule bottlenecks;
- service-category demand;
- probable operational overruns.

Forecasts must identify themselves as forecasts and should expose confidence/context where practical. MASS shall not present weak projections as known facts.

## 6. Customer intelligence

V3 may develop a deeper Customer 360 through:

- customer lifetime value indicators;
- retention probability;
- repeat-service propensity;
- equipment/property service patterns;
- behavioral segmentation;
- communication responsiveness patterns;
- likely future service needs;
- relationship-health indicators.

These systems must preserve privacy, permissions, and appropriate explainability.

## 7. Inventory intelligence

V3 may forecast inventory needs using combinations of:

- historical consumption;
- scheduled jobs;
- Pipeline demand;
- service mix;
- seasonality;
- geographic demand;
- supplier lead time.

Outputs may include predicted stockouts, recommended purchase timing, optimized reorder quantities, and demand-linked stocking recommendations.

## 8. Communications intelligence

With appropriate provider capability and governance, V3 may derive structured intelligence from RingCentral and other communications evidence, including:

- conversation classification;
- probable contact outcome;
- customer intent;
- promises/commitments detected in conversation;
- follow-up requirements;
- escalation indicators;
- sentiment/context where appropriate;
- suggested Pipeline reconciliation.

A transcript or recording is evidence. Model inference must be distinguishable from confirmed human/system facts. High-impact workflow mutations should remain governed.

## 9. Predictive Pulse

Pulse should evolve across generations:

- **V1:** something is happening / requires attention now.
- **V2:** a governed rule or threshold explains why attention is required.
- **V3:** MASS can identify that a condition is likely to become important before the threshold is actually crossed.

Example:

> Schedule Risk — Amber  
> Current schedule is within policy, but accumulated travel/job-duration patterns indicate a high likelihood that the 3:30 PM appointment will fall outside its service window.

Predictive Pulse must remain restrained, explainable, confidence-aware, and subordinate to the signal budget. Prediction is not permission to turn every uncertainty into an alarm.

## 10. Adaptive operational recommendations

V3 may use learned history to recommend actions across Pipeline, Dispatch, Calendar, Inventory, customer lifecycle, and growth intelligence.

Recommendations should answer, where practical:

- what is recommended;
- why;
- what evidence/pattern contributed;
- confidence or uncertainty;
- expected impact;
- whether the action is advisory or automatically authorized.

## 11. Growth intelligence

V3 may deepen operational growth analysis through learned relationships such as:

- source quality beyond raw conversion;
- response-time impact;
- review likelihood;
- referral propensity;
- retention patterns;
- promotion effectiveness;
- service/customer cohort behavior;
- geographic opportunity patterns.

The existing product boundary remains: MASS may understand and operationalize growth arising from the service lifecycle without becoming a general-purpose advertising/creative/publishing platform.

## 12. Advanced technician intelligence

V3 may provide:

- efficiency trends;
- predicted completion performance;
- technician/service affinity;
- coaching indicators;
- workload-risk prediction;
- learned route/territory performance;
- customer-experience outcome correlations;
- richer performance intelligence.

Intelligence should be used to improve operations and coaching, not create opaque or arbitrary employee scoring.

## 13. Cross-system search and command intelligence

V3 may evolve universal search into contextual MASS intelligence capable of locating and reasoning across customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, communications, inventory, settings, rules, events, and operational history.

Responses/actions must honor role permissions and distinguish retrieved facts from inferred conclusions.

## 14. Explainability and governance

V3 materially increases the importance of audit and explanation.

MASS should preserve evidence of:

- model/recommendation version where relevant;
- recommendation or prediction;
- supporting context/features at an appropriate level;
- confidence/uncertainty where meaningful;
- human acceptance/rejection;
- resulting outcome;
- later model evaluation where implemented.

Users must be able to understand why consequential recommendations appeared.

## 15. Learning loop

The long-term V3 flywheel is:

**History + Current State + Governed Rules → Prediction → Recommended Intervention → Human/Authorized Action → Outcome → Learning Evidence.**

Learning systems should be evaluated against real outcomes and should not silently change critical operational policy.

## 16. Autonomy boundary

Predictive intelligence does not automatically grant execution authority.

There is a major distinction between:

> MASS believes X is likely to happen.

and

> MASS is authorized to act on X without human approval.

Broad autonomous operations are intentionally outside this V3 baseline. If MASS later earns bounded autonomous authority, that should be governed as a separate future/V4-class doctrine with explicit permissions, reversibility, risk controls, and human override.

## 17. Governing principle

> **V3 shall make MASS anticipatory without making it reckless. Intelligence must be grounded in operational truth, constrained by V2 governance, explainable to authorized users, and clearly distinguished from confirmed facts.**
