# MASS Future Reference Doctrine — Governed Autonomy

**Doctrine ID:** MASS-FUTURE-DOCTRINE-001  
**Target:** Future / V4 Candidate  
**Classification:** Deferred Strategic Boundary  
**Status:** Reserved — Not Authorized for Implementation  
**Established:** 2026-08-22

## Purpose

This document preserves a boundary identified during V1–V3 planning: predictive intelligence and autonomous execution are not the same capability.

V3 may allow MASS to learn, anticipate, predict, and recommend. It does **not** inherently authorize MASS to take broad consequential actions without human approval.

A future governed-autonomy generation may be considered only after MASS has demonstrated:

- reliable V1 operational truth;
- mature V2 configuration, permissions, policy, audit, rollback, and deterministic automation;
- sufficiently evaluated V3 intelligence;
- clear authorization boundaries;
- reversible execution where practical;
- human override;
- safety and failure containment;
- explainability and audit evidence;
- measured operational benefit.

Potential future examples could include narrowly authorized automatic requeue, capacity balancing, inventory actions, customer lifecycle execution, or dispatch adjustments. Each autonomous capability would require explicit scope and acceptance criteria rather than inheriting authority from an AI recommendation system.

## Governing principle

> **Knowing what should probably happen is not the same as being authorized to make it happen. Autonomy must be earned, bounded, auditable, and reversible.**
