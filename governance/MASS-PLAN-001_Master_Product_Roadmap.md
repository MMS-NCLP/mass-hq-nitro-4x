## 1. Purpose

MASS-PLAN-001 preserves the complete intended product shape of MASS so manufacturing never depends on memory, chat history, or improvised sequencing.

It answers:

1. What MASS intends to become.
2. Which major products and libraries belong to the platform.
3. What each product is responsible for.
4. What has been completed, what is active, and what remains.
5. What may enter production next.

This roadmap governs product inclusion and manufacturing order. The Constitution governs authority. Engineering specifications govern shared platform behavior. Application packages govern implementation domains.

## 2. Permanent Product Principle

MASS is a business operating system that continuously converts organizational complexity into clear, well-supported execution while leaving the executive to make simpler decisions.

MASS is not intended to replace every specialist platform. It coordinates the systems, people, intelligence, evidence, and responsibilities required to move approved work toward reliable completion.

The product must first do the promised thing reliably, then improve with operating data, refine with experience, and expand through earned revenue and investment.

## 3. Production Law

Authorized production continues until Executive Authority explicitly pauses it.

- The production inbox shall remain supplied with approved work whenever roadmap-authorized work remains.
- Architecture, governance, templates, and manufacturing standards may improve in parallel with production.
- Discovery of missing documentation or planning artifacts does not stop the conveyor unless an architecture-critical defect threatens platform integrity.
- No AI participant may independently pause, close, redirect, or deprioritize authorized production.
- No APP, Engine, Library, Marketplace component, or major product family may enter manufacturing until it has a canonical entry in MASS-PLAN-001.
- Work orders are manufactured from the canonical repository inbox, not reconstructed from memory.

## 7. Plugin & Installed Intelligence Ecosystem

The original plugin vision is a first-class product family and shall not be reduced to ordinary integrations.

A MASS plugin is an installable, governed capability package that can add organizational intelligence, professional knowledge, workflows, templates, data contracts, agents, automations, dashboards, forms, and system connections without changing the platform core.

### Plugin Framework Responsibilities

- Plugin manifest and identity
- Capability discovery and registration
- Installation, activation, suspension, removal, and update lifecycle
- Semantic versioning and compatibility
- Dependencies and conflict resolution
- Tenant permissions and minimum necessary access
- Input/output contracts
- Event subscriptions and publications
- API and gateway contracts
- AI-callable skills and tool contracts
- Human approval and execution boundaries
- Package signing, trust, provenance, and certification
- Internal, organization-specific, partner, and third-party plugins
- Testing, health, observability, rollback, and deprecation
- Marketplace readiness

## 17. Change Control

MASS-PLAN-001 is living, but not casual.

Changes must:

- Preserve the original product promise.
- Record why an APP, library, pack, or platform family was added, renamed, split, merged, deferred, or retired.
- Distinguish planned scope from manufactured scope.
- Never rewrite completed history to create the appearance of prior planning.
- Never remove an original concept merely because it is not achievable in V1.
- Preserve room for future capability while labeling V1 honestly.

The roadmap shall remain readable to the executive, future maintainers, Codex, Claude, and other authorized production participants.
