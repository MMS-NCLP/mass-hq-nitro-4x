# Universal Preface

## Governing Interpretation Standard for the Momentum Marketing Solutions Ecosystem

### Version 1.0.0 — Approved

---

## Constitutional Authority

This document derives its authority from the Master Governing Mission Statement and serves as the authoritative interpretation standard for all subordinate governing documents.

## Purpose

This Universal Preface establishes how every governing document within the Momentum Marketing Solutions ecosystem shall be read, interpreted, and applied.

It does not define what the ecosystem builds. The Master Governing Mission Statement defines purpose. Platform constitutions define responsibility. Engineering packages define implementation. This document defines interpretation — the rules by which all other governing documents are understood.

Every governing document produced under this ecosystem — constitutions, governance decision records, strategic governance manuals, domain dictionaries, engineering work orders, engineering packages, and production artifacts — shall be interpreted in accordance with the principles established here.

## Interpretation Principles

### Documents mean what they say.

Governing language shall be interpreted by its plain meaning. When a governing document makes a statement, establishes a rule, or defines a boundary, it means exactly that — not a looser version that would be more convenient for a particular implementation.

If a governing document's language produces an impractical outcome, the remedy is a governed revision — not a creative reinterpretation.

### Silence is not permission.

If a governing document does not address a particular activity, that silence does not authorize the activity. Unaddressed activities shall be escalated through the authority chain for explicit determination rather than resolved through assumption.

### Authority flows downward. It does not flow upward.

A subordinate document may not grant itself authority that its governing parent does not provide. An engineering package may not override a governance decision record. An implementation may not contradict its engineering work order. A work order may not exceed the scope established by the governance framework it cites.

If a conflict exists between documents at different levels of the authority chain, the higher-authority document governs until a proper revision reconciles the conflict.

### Specificity governs within its scope.

When a higher-authority document establishes a general principle and a lower-authority document provides specific guidance within its authorized scope, the specific guidance governs for that scope — provided it does not contradict the general principle.

A platform constitution may establish marketplace rules more specific than the Mission Statement's governing values, but those rules must remain consistent with the values they implement.

### Intent survives imprecision.

Governing documents are written by people. When language is ambiguous, interpretation shall favor the intent most consistent with the governing parent's established purpose and principles rather than the interpretation most convenient for the reader.

No interpretation may expand the delegated authority of the implementing party.

When intent itself is unclear, the matter shall be escalated for executive determination rather than resolved by the implementing party.

## Document Authority

### Every document has a governing parent.

No governing document exists in isolation. Every document shall identify the document from which it derives its authority. If a document cannot identify its governing parent, it is not yet a governing document — it is a draft awaiting placement in the authority chain.

### Every document has a defined scope.

Governing documents shall state what they govern. A document that governs marketplace trust principles does not implicitly govern engineering implementation standards. Scope shall be explicit, not inherited by proximity or naming convention.

### Documents are artifacts, not conversations.

Once a governing document is approved, it becomes an artifact of record. Its authority derives from its approved state, not from the discussions that preceded it. Conversations, meeting notes, chat transcripts, and planning sessions may provide context but do not carry governing authority unless formally incorporated through an approved revision.

## Revision and Continuity

### Governance evolves through revision, not replacement.

When governing principles change, the change shall be recorded through the appropriate governance mechanism — a new governance decision record, an approved amendment, or a versioned revision. The original record shall be preserved.

Governance history is not rewritten. It is extended.

### Governing documents shall not be silently modified.

Changes to governing documents require explicit authorization from the document's designated revision authority. Administrative corrections (typographical errors, formatting, broken references) are permitted. Substantive changes to governing language, scope, boundaries, or principles require formal revision.

### Superseded documents remain part of the record.

When a governing document is superseded by a newer version or a subsequent governance decision, the original document is retained as a historical artifact. Its metadata shall record what superseded it and when. The governance history remains complete and auditable.

## Application

### This preface applies universally.

Every governing document produced within the Momentum Marketing Solutions ecosystem — across all platforms, all governance levels, all engineering domains, and all production activities — shall be interpreted in accordance with this Universal Preface.

Platform-specific interpretation standards may supplement this preface within their authorized scope but may not contradict it.

### This preface governs itself.

The Universal Preface is itself a governing document. It derives its authority from the Master Governing Mission Statement and is subject to the same revision, immutability, and interpretation principles it establishes.

### This preface is intentionally brief.

Interpretation standards lose authority when they require additional interpretation merely to be understood. This document establishes principles, not procedures. Procedures belong in subordinate governance and engineering documents.

If a question of interpretation cannot be resolved by the principles stated here, the matter shall be escalated through the authority chain to the appropriate governing body for determination.
